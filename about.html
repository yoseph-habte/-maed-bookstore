About Maed

Maed (ማእድ) started as a small idea between a few book lovers who wanted an easier way for people to find good books without the hassle of going store to store. Today we bring that same idea online.

Our Story

We started this project because we noticed most people around us either didn't have easy access to a wide variety of books, or didn't have time to go looking for them. So we built Maed to bring the bookstore experience to your screen, whether you're looking for a textbook, a novel, or something to read over the weekend.
